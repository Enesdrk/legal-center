Introduction

Thank you for using CSecret Nades. We respect your privacy and explain here how information is handled while using the app.

This app is an independent guide for Counter-Strike players and is not affiliated with Valve Corporation.

CSecret Nades is free to download on iOS and Android. The app may show advertisements and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (“Remove Ads”) to disable ads.

Information We Process

Data we do not collect directly

We do not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Data stored on your device

The app may store preferences on your device (for example, language, favorites, and whether Remove Ads is active) using on-device storage such as local app data.

Advertising (iOS and Android)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data to deliver ads, measure ad performance, limit ad frequency, and prevent fraud.

On iOS, the system may show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use data for personalized ads and cross-app tracking as defined by Apple. If you deny tracking, ads may still appear but may be less personalized. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partners

In-app purchases

If you purchase Remove Ads, payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase and entitlement status are validated through the store and our purchase partner RevenueCat so ad-free access can be applied and restored on your devices when you use Restore Purchases.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

App Store transaction information (iOS only)

On iOS, the app may use Apple StoreKit App Transaction information (such as original app version and original purchase date) only to determine whether you acquired the app during a previous paid period and should continue to receive an ad-free experience without purchasing Remove Ads again. This uses Apple’s APIs on your device; we do not use it for advertising.

User-provided information

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your email address and message content) only to respond to you.

Use of Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the app and its features.
  • To display advertisements on iOS and Android.
  • To deliver, validate, and restore the Remove Ads in-app purchase and apply ad-free access when entitled.
  • To apply legacy ad-free access for eligible users who purchased the app on iOS before it became free, where supported by Apple purchase records and app logic.
  • To diagnose technical issues and help prevent abuse or fraud related to ads and purchases.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — in-app purchases and transaction verification on iOS (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases on Android (Google Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation and entitlement sync (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)

We do not operate our own analytics SDK (such as Firebase Analytics) in the app.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are applied to protect information processed by the app and its third-party service providers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 12 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data Retention and Deletion

CSecret Nades does not require users to create an account and does not maintain personal user profiles on our servers.

Locally stored app preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Information processed by AdMob, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat is retained according to those providers’ policies and your device or account settings.

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings (iOS Tracking settings; Android Google ads settings). For purchases, use your Apple or Google account purchase history and Restore Purchases in the app.

If you have questions about privacy or the handling of information in connection with the app, you can contact us using the information below.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy and change the update date when modifications occur. Material changes may also be noted in the app where appropriate.
