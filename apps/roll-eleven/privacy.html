1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Roll Eleven (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our mobile application (“App”) on iOS and Android.

Roll Eleven is designed primarily as an offline-first game. We minimize data collection and do not require you to create an account.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data on your device only:

  • Game progress and Hall of Fame records
  • App preferences (language, sound, and similar settings)
  • Legal disclaimer acceptance status
  • In-app purchase entitlement flags (for example, Remove Ads)
  • Ad-related counters used for frequency controls (for example, interstitial timing)

This data remains on your device unless you delete the App or use the in-app reset options.

2.2 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

When ads or purchases are enabled, third parties may collect limited technical data, such as:

  • Device identifiers (for example, advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewards)
  • Purchase transaction metadata handled by the app store

We do not directly collect your name, email address, or phone number through the App.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide core gameplay, draft, and simulation features
  • Remember your preferences and local progress
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Process optional in-app purchases
  • Improve stability, performance, and user experience
  • Comply with legal and platform requirements

5. Advertising

Google AdMob

Roll Eleven displays ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices.

You can limit ad tracking through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Purchasing the optional Remove Ads in-app product disables ad display in supported areas of the App.

6. In-App Purchases

If you buy optional products (such as Remove Ads), payment and transaction validation are handled by the relevant app store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only:

  • With service providers that help operate ads or purchases
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • To protect our rights, users, or the security of the App

8. Data Retention

  • Local app data: kept on your device until deleted by you
  • Ad and store provider data: retained according to each provider’s policy

You can delete local data using the in-app reset options or by uninstalling the App.

9. Children’s Privacy

Roll Eleven is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. International Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or other regions with privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your information.

Where consent is required for advertising, we rely on Google UMP and applicable platform consent flows.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
